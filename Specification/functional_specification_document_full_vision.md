## 1. Project Overview

### Project Title
**Partner Portal / Coordination Software for Partner Sales Models**

### Core Idea
The system goes beyond classic PRM (Partner Relationship Management). It does not only manage the relationship between company and partners, but also supports **product-based and shop-based resale through partners**. It bundles central partner-related sales processes — partner management, product management, product release, partner-driven ordering, an external partner-branded shop, and commission logic — in one platform, closing the gap between classic PRM and product-related partner-shop logic.

### Core Model
**Company → Partner → End Customer**

### Reference Client
Reference client (unnamed) — ~200 partners, ~800 articles. Partner orders currently arrive by e-mail (~10–15/day) and phone (~3–4/day), processed manually. (PAPO is this project's own name, not the client.)

### Full Project Goal
Develop a web-based system that:
- manages partners centrally (incl. per-partner commission level, with automatic level-up by sales threshold)
- manages products/articles centrally
- allows the company to release products for selected partners
- gives partners their own dashboard and internal management area
- allows partners to build an **external, partner-branded shop** from released products
- allows end customers to **order directly through partner shops**
- assigns every sale to the correct partner automatically, whether placed internally by the partner or externally by an end customer
- represents full commission logic between company and partner, including settlement/payout
- sends real, automated order and shipping confirmations
- makes administrative and sales processes transparent and efficient across the whole chain

## 2. Roles (Full Vision)

### Company / Manufacturer / Operator
Central instance. Manages articles/products; manages partners; releases products for partners; defines price, description and availability; receives/processes orders from both internal partner entry and the external partner shop; handles fulfillment/delivery to end customers; handles billing/commission assignment and settlement/payout.

### Partner / Sales Partner
Has master data. Receives released products. Can build a **partner-branded external shop** from released products (own logo, colors, shop name, choice of layout variant). Sees relevant data in a dashboard. Places internal orders on a customer's behalf **and/or** receives orders/inquiries generated by end customers through their own shop. Receives commission, with automatic level-up as sales volume crosses defined thresholds. Does **not** define central product data, prices, or descriptions — always uses company-defined product data.

### End Customer
In the full vision, End Customer is a primary user, not just a secondary one:
- interacts with the **external partner shop**, not the internal partner portal
- sees released products in the partner's branded shop
- orders directly (Phase 2) — MVP has the partner order on their behalf instead
- is assigned to a partner in the background via the shop they ordered through
- receives delivery and confirmations from the company, with the partner as commercial point of contact

## 3. Two Areas (Full Vision)

### Internal Area — Company ↔ Partner
Part of the partner portal, not a full second shop. Internal catalog, management and release area. Includes partner management, partner master data, article/product data, product release, dashboard functions, partner-related process visibility, billing/commission reference (incl. settlement history), and internal coordination. Partners can also enter orders here manually on a customer's behalf (this internal ordering path is what the MVP builds first, and it remains available even once the external shop exists).

### External Area — Partner → End Customer
The **external partner shop** (Phase 2, B-01/B-02). Visible to end customers. Based on centrally maintained products; shows only released products. Customizable in partner design (logo, colors, shop name, choice of ≥2 layout variants) — central product data (price/description) stays locked regardless of shop customization. Allows direct ordering by the end customer. Assigns the sale to the partner automatically. Leads to fulfillment by the company in the background, same as internally-entered orders.

## 4. Difference from Classic PRM

Classic PRM systems typically focus on partner onboarding, deal registration, leads, incentives/commissions, reporting, enablement/training and content sharing.

This project's full vision expands that with:
- central article/product management
- product-based partner releases
- **partner-specific external shop creation** with design customization
- clear separation between internal partner portal and external partner shop
- direct connection between partner shop, end-customer order, and commission assignment
- automatic commission level-up as an incentive mechanism tied directly to sales volume

## 6. Use Cases (Full Vision, Beyond MVP)

### UC-A: End Customer Orders Through a Partner's External Shop (Phase 2, B-01)
An end customer visits a partner's branded shop URL, browses the products the company has enabled for that partner, adds items to a cart, and places a direct order — no login, no partner involvement at order time. The order is automatically assigned to the partner whose shop it came through, exactly like an internally-entered order, and enters the same submitted → reviewed → fulfilled pipeline. Confirmation e-mails go out to the end customer directly (real, not simulated — B-04).

### UC-B: Partner Customizes Their Shop (Phase 2, B-02)
A partner logs into the internal area and configures their external shop's logo, color scheme, shop name, and picks one of the provided layout variants. Central product data (price, description, availability) remains company-controlled and cannot be edited by the partner regardless of shop customization.

### UC-C: Partner Crosses a Commission Threshold (Phase 2, B-03)
As a partner's cumulative sales cross a defined threshold, the system automatically promotes them to the next commission level and applies the new percentage to subsequent orders, without company intervention. (Thresholds per level are an open question to define before this is built.)

### UC-D: Company Settles Commission (Phase 2, B-07)
The company reviews accumulated, unsettled commission per partner over a period, marks it as paid out, and the system records the settlement so it's excluded from future settlement runs. (MVP only shows commission transparently — own record per order + dashboard sums — with no settlement step.)

### UC-E: Shop Inquiry Auto-Forwards to Partner (Phase 2, B-05)
An inquiry submitted through the external shop (not yet a full order — e.g. a question or quote request) is automatically routed to the responsible partner. Depends on B-01 existing first.

## 7. Data Model — Full Vision

Same core entities as the MVP; the full vision does not require new entity types, only extended behavior on existing ones:

- **Partner** — partner identity, **commission level with auto-promotion** (Phase 2 adds threshold-tracking logic), active/inactive status, **shop design settings** (Phase 2: logo, colors, name, layout variant reference).
- **Product** — company-managed article data (unchanged from MVP).
- **ProductEnablement** — links products to partners (unchanged from MVP; also drives what's visible in the external shop).
- **PartnerShop** — (Phase 2, new logical concept, not present in MVP) shop configuration for a partner; 1 partner : 1 shop.
- **Kunde (End Customer)** — reusable end-customer record; in Phase 2, can also be created directly by an end customer's own external-shop order rather than only by a partner.
- **Order / OrderItem** — same shape as MVP; source distinguishes internal-partner-entry vs. external-shop-direct in Phase 2 (both funnel into the same status pipeline).
- **CommissionRecord** — same as MVP; Phase 2 adds a **settlement/payout record** linking multiple CommissionRecords to a payout event (B-07).
